How Delegation Works
  • If a selected approver has an active delegation today, the system assigns the approval step to the delegate.
  • Delegation applies only between start date and end date.
  • Only active users in the same organization can be selected.
  • Organization Admin can manage all delegations.
  • Approvers can create their own delegation.
Example
  • Approver A is on leave from 10 May to 15 May.
  • Approver A delegates to Approver B.
  • Any approval assigned to A during this period goes to B.
