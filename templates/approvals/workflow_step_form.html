Assignment Logic
  • Fixed approver has highest priority.
  • Department-based assigns the request department head.
  • If both are empty, system assigns by required role.
  • If no user is found, organization admin is used as fallback.
  • Do not select fixed approver and department-based together.
SLA Rules
  • SLA hours controls when a step becomes overdue.
  • Reminder before hours must be less than SLA hours.
  • Overdue steps are escalated to department head or organization admin.
  • Celery Beat checks SLA status every 15 minutes.
Example Flow
  • Step 1: Department Head — 24 hours SLA
  • Step 2: Approver — 48 hours SLA
  • Step 3: Organization Admin as final step
